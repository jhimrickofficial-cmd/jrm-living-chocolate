More Than Chocolate. An Experience Worth Savoring.

At Living Chocolate, cacao is more than an ingredient. It is an invitation to slow down, explore extraordinary flavors, and reconnect with the simple pleasure of chocolate.

Nestled in Sedona, Arizona, our chocolate shop and café brings together raw cacao creations, chocolate treats, and thoughtfully prepared drinks for curious chocolate lovers and everyday sweet moments.
